Where the vans come from

Ex-fleet describes how a van was used before. The vans are entered by dealers, traders, businesses and other commercial vendors, and each listing states the provenance where it is known.

That is why the listing matters more than the label. Registration, mileage, MOT status, service history, number of keys, V5C status and fitted equipment are listed where the vendor supplies them, with photographs of the cab, bodywork and load area.

Ex-lease and contract hire returns come through as well, alongside vans from businesses replacing or reducing their own.

How buying at an ex-fleet van auction works

Four steps, from registering to collecting what you have won.

  1. Register to bid

    Registration is free. Manual approval and identification may be required before an account can bid, so register ahead of the sale you are watching.

  2. Read the lot and set your maximum

    Each lot carries the vendor's description and photographs, the van's location, its VAT treatment, and the buyer's premium on its Additional Fees tab. Add them up and decide your ceiling before the lot closes.

  3. Bid in the timed online auction

    Bidding normally remains open for several days, with the exact opening and closing times stated for each auction. In our current timed-auction format, a bid in the last ten minutes extends that lot by ten minutes, so nobody wins on the buzzer.

  4. Pay and collect

    We invoice you after the sale, payable in full by bank transfer within 24 hours. Once your payment clears we release the collection details, and you collect from the stated location by appointment.

Before you bid

Check the van, its history and collection arrangements

Use the registration to research the van's recorded history, alongside the seller's description, photographs and your own inspection. Do the checks first, and bid on what the van needs as well as what it is.

Viewings are available by appointment and are strongly recommended. Universal Auctions Group acts as auctioneer and agent for the seller, with the sale contract between the seller and successful buyer.

Check the official MOT history and DVLA record against the registration, compare recorded mileages with the odometer reading and service information supplied, and make your own finance, title and insurance-history enquiries. Look for signs of removed fleet equipment, check the keys and any fitted racking, and make sure the van suits any emissions zone you work in.

Allow for servicing, tyres, any repairs, transport and taxation in your maximum. Buyers are responsible for arranging collection and transport. Any delivery option specifically stated within a lot is provided by the seller and subject to separate agreement.

What does ex-fleet mean?

Ex-fleet describes a van's previous use, for example with a company, utility, rental or leasing fleet. It is not a condition grade or a guarantee of service history, and a van may have changed hands before it reaches auction, so take the provenance from each listing.

Where can I buy ex-fleet vans without going through a dealer forecourt?

At trade auctions. Universal Auctions Group runs trade-only timed online auctions of ex-fleet, ex-lease and business-use vans, so registered business buyers bid online and the price is set by open bidding.

What is the difference between ex-fleet and ex-lease vans?

Ex-lease vans have come back at the end of a contract hire or lease agreement. Ex-fleet is the wider label for vans that worked in a company, utility or rental fleet. Both come up in our auctions, and each listing states the provenance where it is known.

What checks should I do before bidding on a van?

Check the MOT history and DVLA record against the registration, compare the mileages, and make your own finance, title and insurance-history enquiries. Where a registration number is available, Universal Auctions Group carries out the vehicle data check described in our terms before the vehicle is listed. It is not a mechanical inspection, title guarantee or substitute for the buyer's own enquiries.

What will a lot cost me on top of the hammer price?

A 20% buyer's premium plus VAT is payable in addition to the hammer price. VAT on the hammer price applies where stated in the lot description. Buyers must also allow for collection and transport costs.

How quickly do I have to pay and collect?

Payment is due in full by bank transfer within 24 hours of the invoice. Collection is by appointment once your payment has cleared, within the period stated in that sale's terms. Confirm collection arrangements before bidding. If driving the van away, ensure it meets all road-use requirements; otherwise arrange suitable transport.

Can I sell vans through you?

Yes, from one van to a yard of them. We sell for dealers, traders, businesses, lenders and administrators. Vans are sold from your premises and buyers collect after paying. Talk to us about the vans and your timetable.

Are vans sold with a warranty?

Lots are sold as seen, where lying. Descriptions and photographs come from the vendor, and mileage and history are not warranted unless the lot expressly says so, so form your own view of each van before you bid.
