How buying at auction works

Our sales are timed and online, so you can bid from anywhere. Three things to know before your first sale.

Register, then do your homework

Registering to bid may need approval and identification, so do it in advance. Then read the lot, check the fees on it, and arrange a viewing if you want one. We do not inspect the lots, so that work is yours.

Bid to a number you set first

Lots close at published times, a few seconds apart. A valid bid in a lot's last ten minutes extends that lot by ten minutes, so nobody wins on the buzzer. Bids are binding and cannot be withdrawn.

Pay, then collect

Win a lot and we invoice you for bank transfer. Once the payment clears you collect from the seller's premises, by appointment. We can get you a transport quote before you bid.

Why sell with UAG

Selling with no fees and no commission

Our sellers pay us nothing. There is no listing fee, no entry fee and no commission taken out of what your assets make. Your stock stays on your own site for the whole sale, and the money reaches you before anything is collected.

  • No fees, no commission

    You receive 100% of the hammer price together with any applicable VAT, with no commission deducted and no listing or entry fee to pay.

  • Stock stays on your site

    Lots remain on your premises for the whole sale, listed from your own details and photographs. Nothing is moved before it is sold.

  • Paid before collection

    The buyer pays us by bank transfer. Payment reaches you ordinarily within 24 hours of the funds clearing, and collection is only authorised after that.

Liquidation and administration

Asset disposal for administrators and receivers

We sell plant, vehicles and equipment for administrators, receivers and insolvency practitioners. Sales run online and reach trade buyers, dealers and exporters, the assets stay at the company's premises until they are sold and paid for, and you get a settlement record lot by lot.

  • Lot-by-lot reporting

    What each asset made, what was invoiced and what was paid, so the schedule reconciles line by line.

  • A record of how the market was tested

    The marketing record and the bidding activity each lot attracted. A properly marketed auction is strong supporting evidence, though no sale route is a legal guarantee on its own.

  • A valuation for the file if you need one

    An independent professional valuation can be arranged alongside the achieved figures.
