How buying at auction with us works

Six steps, from registering to driving it away.

  1. Register to bid

    Set up an account and register for the sale. Accounts are approved before bidding opens, so do it ahead of the close.

  2. Read the lot

    Each lot page carries the seller's description and photographs, the VAT treatment, and the buyer's premium and internet surcharge that apply, on its Additional Fees tab.

  3. View it if you want to

    Any lot can be viewed by appointment. Worth doing on anything mechanical: you look the machine over yourself and bid on what you have seen.

  4. Set your maximum and bid

    Work out your all-in figure, enter it as a maximum bid, and the platform bids up to it for you. You often win below your ceiling.

  5. Pay by bank transfer

    We invoice you after the sale, payable in full by bank transfer within 24 hours.

  6. Collect by appointment

    Once your payment clears we send the collection details. You collect from the seller's premises at a booked time, or we can quote you for transport.

The closing rule

Nobody wins on the buzzer

Lots close at published times, a few seconds apart. A bid in a lot's final ten minutes extends that lot by ten minutes from the bid, and it extends again each time another bid is accepted.

The lot stays open while anyone still wants it, so it is won on price rather than on timing or a fast connection. If you get outbid late, you get the chance to come back.

That rewards deciding your number in advance. Enter it as a maximum bid and the platform holds it through every extension, whether you are watching or not. A bid is a commitment to buy, so the number you set beforehand is the number that counts.

Overseas buyers

Buy from anywhere, and export it

Online bidding means you can buy a UK lot without leaving your own country, and we are EORI registered, so overseas trade buyers are a normal part of our sales.

On an export sale the VAT is taken as a deposit and refunded once you send us acceptable evidence that the goods have left the UK, within 28 days of the invoice. Tell us you are exporting before the sale and we will agree the paperwork and the timings with you in advance.

UK plant, machinery and commercial vehicles travel well, and a wider pool of bidders is part of what brings good stock through our sales in the first place.

Questions buyers ask

How do I know what a lot will cost me?

Everything is on the lot page before you bid: the buyer's premium and internet surcharge on the Additional Fees tab, and the VAT treatment in the description. Add them to the hammer price you have in mind, add transport, and that is your ceiling. Ask us for a transport figure and every part of the sum is a real number.

Can I see a machine before I bid on it?

Yes, on any lot. Send us the lot number and we will arrange a time with the seller. On anything mechanical we would encourage it, and where you cannot get there we can go back to the seller for more photographs or a particular detail.

When do I pay, and how?

We invoice you after the sale and payment is by bank transfer, in full, within 24 hours of the invoice. Once it clears we send you the collection details.

What happens at the end of a lot?

A bid in the last ten minutes extends the lot by ten minutes, so it stays open while anyone still wants it. Where the bidding finishes below the seller's reserve, we put your highest bid to them and come back to you, so a lot that closes without selling is not always the end of it.

Can you help me get it home?

We can obtain a no-obligation transport quotation on any lot. Send the lot number and your postcode, ideally before you bid, and tell us whether you can offload at your end. You are free to use your own haulier instead.

I want to bid on two similar lots. What should I do?

Bid on the one you want first, and move to the second only once you have lost the first. Each winning bid is a separate commitment to buy, so a maximum left on three similar lots can win you all three.

Do I need to be in the UK?

No. Bidding is online and overseas trade buyers are a normal part of our sales. We are EORI registered, and on an export sale the VAT is taken as a deposit and refunded on acceptable evidence of export within 28 days of the invoice.
