Can you reach overseas buyers?

Yes. Online bidding opens the sale to trade buyers anywhere, and overseas trade buyers are welcome to participate without attending.

A wider pool of bidders tends to lift the price, and assets with thin demand here sometimes have strong demand abroad. Export buyers arrange their own transport and export documentation, and pay a VAT deposit that is refunded only when satisfactory export evidence reaches both you and us within 28 days of the invoice, so your position is unaffected.

Well-specified assets with a clear history travel best, which is another reason the detail you send us at the start is worth getting right.
