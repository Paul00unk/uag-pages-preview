Your stock never leaves your site until it sells

Lots are listed where they stand, from your own details and photographs. Nothing is moved, nothing goes into anyone else's yard, and nothing leaves until it is sold and paid for.

There is no transport bill before a sale and no storage bill during one. If a machine is still earning while the sale runs, it can keep earning. Any amendment or withdrawal must be agreed with UAG and remains subject to the agreed seller terms.

Listings are based on the details and photographs you supply, and we prepare and check each one before it goes live. We may amend, clarify or reject information to keep a listing accurate and compliant, so what goes in front of buyers is both your knowledge of the asset and a listing that stands up.
