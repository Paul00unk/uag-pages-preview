Why sell with UAG

Selling with us costs you nothing

There are no standard selling, listing, entry or commission charges. You receive 100% of the hammer price together with any applicable VAT.

There is no percentage coming off the top of what your assets make. The charges that fund the sale sit on the buyer's side of the invoice, on top of the hammer price, so what your lot makes is what reaches you.

That matters most on the assets you were least sure about. A machine that makes less than you hoped still makes it all, because there is no percentage coming off the top and no minimum to clear before you see anything.
