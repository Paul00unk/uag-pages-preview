Why sell with UAG

Selling with us costs you nothing

There are no standard selling, listing, entry or commission charges. You receive 100% of the hammer price together with any applicable VAT.

There is no percentage coming off the top of what your assets make. The charges that fund the sale sit on the buyer's side of the invoice, on top of the hammer price, so what your lot makes is what reaches you.

That matters most on the assets you were least sure about. A machine that makes less than you hoped still makes it all, because there is no percentage coming off the top and no minimum to clear before you see anything.

Your stock never leaves your site until it sells

Lots are listed where they stand, from your own details and photographs. Nothing is moved, nothing goes into anyone else's yard, and nothing leaves until it is sold and paid for.

There is no transport bill before a sale and no storage bill during one. If a machine is still earning while the sale runs, it can keep earning. Any amendment or withdrawal must be agreed with UAG and remains subject to the agreed seller terms.

Listings are based on the details and photographs you supply, and we prepare and check each one before it goes live. We may amend, clarify or reject information to keep a listing accurate and compliant, so what goes in front of buyers is both your knowledge of the asset and a listing that stands up.

You are paid before anything is collected

The buyer pays us in full by bank transfer. Payment reaches you ordinarily within 24 hours of the funds clearing, and collection is only authorised after that.

The order matters. Your asset does not leave your site on the strength of a promise, and you are not chasing a buyer for money on something they already have. By the time anyone arrives to load a machine, it has been paid for.

For a business winding down, or an estate that cannot carry a debtor, that sequence is usually the deciding factor rather than the price.

Who bids on your lots

Trade buyers, dealers, contractors, operators, hauliers, farmers and exporters, bidding online from across the UK and overseas. Our sales are trade-only, so the people competing for your asset are businesses that intend to use or resell it.

A local trade sale reaches whoever happens to be nearby. An online sale reaches everyone who wants that machine, wherever they are, and competition between people who all want the same thing is what finds the price.

Overseas trade buyers are welcome to participate, and can bid on a UK asset without attending. On the categories where domestic demand is thin, that second market is often what makes the difference between a lot selling and a lot sitting.

The case for auction is strongest when you have more than one asset, when there is a date you are working to, or when you want to be able to show what everything made and why. The case is weakest on a single low-value item, where the effort outweighs the difference.

A record of what everything made

You get a settlement report lot by lot: what each asset sold for, what was invoiced and what was paid, so the schedule reconciles line by line rather than arriving as one net figure.

Alongside it you get the marketing record and the bidding activity each lot attracted, which shows how the assets were exposed and that real competition took place.

Any owner finds that useful at the end of a year. For an administrator, a receiver or a liquidator it is the point of the exercise, and we cover that side separately in asset disposal for administrators and receivers.
